## 1. Composite string-key lookups reconstructed via `format!()` on every named-type resolution (highest impact)

These are chained through `resolver/precedence.rs:39-66` (`resolve_source_defined_or_known`), which is called from **both** `chain.rs::resolve_props_chain` and `named.rs::resolve_named_uncycled` — i.e. for every prop's type, every generic arg, every extends clause, every union/intersection member, recursively, across the whole component tree. This is the same anti-pattern the codebase has *already* identified and fixed twice elsewhere: `import_map.rs:40-47` explicitly restructured to a nested `FxHashMap<Utf8PathBuf, FxHashMap<CompactString, T>>` specifically "so lookups can borrow `&Utf8Path` directly instead of cloning the whole path per call," and `ResolutionContext::enum_bare_index`/`const_array_bare_index` (resolver/mod.rs:124-135) were added specifically to eliminate a `format!()`-per-candidate scan in `known.rs`. **Fix**: restructure those four maps to nested `FxHashMap<Utf8PathBuf, FxHashMap<CompactString, T>>`, mirroring `ImportResolutionMap`'s established pattern.

## 2. Unconditional deep-clone of every resolved `ComponentEntry` in the sequential output phase

**Fix**: capture `let new_file_path = entry.file_path.clone();` (cheap `Utf8PathBuf` clone) before `entry` moves into `insert`, use that in the diagnostic instead of `entry.file_path`. Drops the clone from O(full ComponentEntry) to O(one path) in the overwhelmingly common non-collision case.
